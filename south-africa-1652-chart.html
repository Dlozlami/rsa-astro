02 · Shape

A chart turned outward

Nine of the ten planets stand above the horizon; Saturn alone is below it, holding the 1st house.

In mundane work the upper half of the chart describes what is public, visible and directed outward, and the lower half describes the ground, the home and what is held. Here almost everything faces outward. The settlement was an outpost serving a company and a trade route whose centre lay elsewhere, and the chart puts nearly all its energy into that outward reach. The one planet left holding the ground is Saturn, the planet of walls, limits and hardship, and it sits in the house of the people themselves.

The 9th house carries the heaviest concentration: Mercury, the Moon, the North Node and the Sun. The 9th is the house of long voyages, shipping, foreign trade, law and religion. The station existed to provision ships on the route between the Netherlands and the East Indies, and the chart places its centre of gravity in exactly that house.

Elements

Modalities

Fire and cardinality dominate: initiative, conquest of new ground, beginnings. Air is almost absent, carried by Pluto alone. Air is the element of exchange, negotiation and shared ideas, and Mercury, the planet that governs exchange, is in its detriment and fall. The founding impulse is strong on action and thin on dialogue.

03 · Angles

The four angles

The Ascendant describes the people and the body of the nation; the Midheaven describes government and authority.

Ascendant · 2°08′ Cancer

A provisioning station

Cancer is the sign of nourishment, shelter and the home port. The Company's instruction was to grow food and supply passing fleets, and its ruler, the Moon, sits in the 9th house of voyages: the people's purpose is to feed the ships.

1st house · Saturn 21°55′ Cancer

Walls first

Saturn in its detriment in the 1st marks the national body with restriction, defence and hardship. Work on the Fort of Good Hope began within the first week. Saturn had stationed direct on 23 March and was still barely moving, which concentrates its weight.

Midheaven · 20°54′ Aries

Authority as conquest

An Aries MC with the exalted Sun 2°42′ from it gives authority a solar, commanding, appointed character. Saturn squares the MC within 1°01′: the people and the governing power are set at right angles from the start.

10th house · Mars 4°05′ Taurus

Force applied to land and cattle

Mars rules the MC and sits in the 10th in Taurus, its detriment. Taurus is land, livestock and fixed property. Government in this chart acts through force over land and cattle.

Descendant · 2°08′ Capricorn

The other, on the threshold

Uranus at 26°29′ Sagittarius stands 5°39′ before the Descendant, opposing the Ascendant. The 7th house is open encounter, treaty and war; disruption arrives through the encounter with others.

IC · 20°54′ Libra

The land, ruled from above

The 4th house of land and territory is empty. Its ruler Venus sits in the 11th, the house of councils, and is the final dispositor of the whole chart (section 04).

04 · Rulership

Every chain ends at the land

Venus in Taurus is the sole final dispositor, and Venus rules the 4th house of land.

A dispositor chain follows each planet to the ruler of the sign it occupies, then to that planet's ruler, until it reaches a planet in its own sign. Using traditional rulers, every planet in this chart resolves to Venus, which sits in its own sign of Taurus. Venus rules the IC and the 4th house: the soil, territory and agriculture. Whatever the chart sets in motion, it is ultimately answerable to the land and its value.

05 · Condition

Two strong planets, four weakened

The Sun and Venus are dignified; Mercury, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are all in detriment or fall.

This is a day chart, since the Sun is above the horizon. The Sun is the light of the sect, Jupiter the benefic of the sect, and Saturn the malefic of the sect; Mars is the malefic contrary to the sect. The Sun is close to its exaltation degree of 19° Aries and also holds the fire triplicity by day, so the light that leads the chart is at full strength.

Both malefics are angular and both are debilitated: Saturn in the 1st in Cancer, Mars in the 10th in Taurus. Angular planets act most visibly, so the two planets of hardship and force are the most prominent players after the Sun. Jupiter, the benefic that should temper them, is in its fall in the 7th and slowing towards its retrograde station on 29 April. Venus, the other benefic, is fully dignified but succedent, in the 11th, working through councils rather than on the angles.

06 · Aspects

The tightest contacts

The closest aspects in the chart all involve the outer planets, Chiron or an angle, which lets the slow forces speak through the faster structure.

Saturn's quincunx to Neptune is exact to within half a degree. Saturn in the 1st and Neptune in the 6th sit in signs that share nothing, so the people's walls and the dissolving pressure in the house of labour and servitude can only adjust to one another, never resolve. Neptune also trines the MC within 0°33′, which gives the governing power an idealised, mythologised face.

Chiron forms a cluster with three key points. It squares the Ascendant within 1°07′, opposes the Moon (the chart ruler) within 1°26′ and is quincunx Mars (the MC ruler) within 0°50′. Chiron is a modern body, discovered in 1977, and is read as the wound that does not close. Here it touches the people, their ruler and the ruler of government all at once.

Pluto in the 12th sextiles the North Node within 0°41′ and is quincunx Jupiter within 0°37′. The 12th is the house of hidden things, confinement and institutions; Pluto there, tied to the Node, sets a concealed transformation into the chart's direction of travel.

07 · The Moon

A balsamic Moon, hours from an eclipse

The Moon is 13°31′ behind the Sun, in its balsamic phase and under the beams, about to be renewed.

The balsamic phase is the last sliver of the lunar cycle, the closing of one cycle before the next begins. The Moon is still inside the Sun's beams (within 17°), so the chart ruler is hidden in the Sun's light. Its next traditional aspect is a square to Jupiter in Capricorn; before that it makes a sextile to Pluto. It is not void of course.

Total solar eclipse at 19°10′ Aries

The New Moon that the chart's Moon is moving towards fell at 11:43 LMT on Monday 8 April 1652, about 23½ hours after the chart moment, and it was a total solar eclipse. It fell 58′ from the chart's Sun and 1°44′ from its Midheaven.

The eclipse was not visible from the Cape. The path of totality crossed Ireland and Scotland, where the day is remembered as Mirk Monday. In mundane tradition, going back to Ptolemy, an eclipse on the angle of a chart is among the strongest signatures it can carry, and this one sits on the Midheaven, the point of authority and government.

09 · History

The first decade against the chart

The settlement's first eight years line up with the chart's strongest signatures.

  1. 1652
    A refreshment station to supply VOC ships; the Fort of Good Hope begun within a week.
    Cancer rising; Moon (chart ruler) in the 9th of shipping; 9th house stellium; Saturn in the 1st.
  2. 1657
    The Company releases its first free burghers and grants them farms along the Liesbeek River.
    Venus, ruler of the 4th (land) and final dispositor, in the 11th (council).
  3. 1658
    The first large shipments of enslaved people arrive: the Amersfoort with captives taken from a Portuguese slaver bound from Angola, then the Hasselt from West Africa.
    Uranus and Neptune in the 6th (servants, labour); 6th ruler Jupiter in fall in the 7th (foreign); Saturn quincunx Neptune.
  4. 1659–60
    The first Khoikhoi–Dutch War, fought over grazing land and cattle.
    Mars in Taurus in the 10th; 7th ruler (open enemies) Saturn in the 1st; Mars quincunx Chiron.
