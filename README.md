# South Africa, 1652

The mundane astrology chart of what-will-be South Africa: the founding of the Dutch East India Company station at Table Bay. The repo holds the chart data, the source charts, and an HTML deep dive into the chart.

## Viewing

Open `south-africa-1652-chart.html` in any browser. It's a single file in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no dependencies. The fonts load from Google Fonts when you're online and fall back to system fonts when you're not. Hover over or tap a planet on the wheel, or a row in the placements table, to isolate its aspects.

## Key findings

- **Venus in Taurus is the sole final dispositor**, and Venus rules the 4th house of land.
- **Saturn rises in Cancer** in the 1st house and squares the Midheaven within 1°01′.
- **The Sun stands on the Midheaven** in Aries, 2°42′ away and close to its exaltation degree.
- **The 9th house holds a stellium:** Mercury, the Moon, the North Node and the Sun, in the house of voyages and trade.
- **A total solar eclipse at 19°10′ Aries** fell about 23½ hours after the chart moment, 1°44′ from the Midheaven. It was "Mirk Monday" in Scotland.

## Sources and method

The **positions** come from the Swiss Ephemeris (Astrodienst AG). They were checked against the Astro.com chart and the Astrodienst ephemeris for 1652.

The **date** comes from the historical record: the ships anchored on 6 April 1652, and Van Riebeeck's command began on 7 April. The **time** was divined by Dlozi Mthethwa.

The **interpretive frameworks** are borrowed from these sources:
- Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos*, for dignities, sect and the use of eclipses.
- William Lilly, *Christian Astrology* (1647).
- Baigent, Campion and Harvey, *Mundane Astrology* (1984).
- Nicholas Campion, *The Book of World Horoscopes*.

The **history** draws on SA History Online and Wikipedia. Full links are in the HTML.
